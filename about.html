About CORAZON

Accurate
Diagnostics.
Better Care.

Corazon Pathology Laboratory is a trusted, modern diagnostic center located in Ramdaspeth, Nagpur, dedicated to delivering accurate, timely, and high-quality laboratory services.

We combine advanced technology with expert medical supervision to ensure reliable diagnostic results that support effective clinical decisions. Our laboratory is equipped with state-of-the-art analyzers, including HPLC systems for HbA1c, automated immunoassay platforms, and precision hematology analyzers.

At Corazon, we believe diagnostics are not just tests — they are the foundation of good healthcare. Our focus is on:

  • Accuracy and reliability
  • Quick turnaround time
  • Patient comfort and convenience
  • Ethical and transparent pricing

We also provide free home sample collection, ensuring that quality diagnostics are accessible to every patient in the comfort of their home.
